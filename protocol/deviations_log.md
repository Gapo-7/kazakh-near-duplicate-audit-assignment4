# Deviations from the frozen protocol

1. **Moodle timing.** The course showed one `as4` submission slot and no separate protocol slot. The team planned to upload the completed materials together. Appendix A and the JSON selection manifest were hashed locally on 5 November 2026 at 08:26:57 UTC, before the first final-sample fetch. They were not uploaded to Moodle before the final runs. This does not meet the brief's Moodle timestamp rule and is stated in the final report.
2. **Download retry only.** After the first final run, Hugging Face Dataset Viewer returned HTTP 429. The data loader was changed to wait longer after rate limits and 1.5 seconds after each new block. No dataset offsets, filters, similarity definitions, thresholds, outcomes, or analysis rules changed. Completed run output and cached blocks were retained.
3. **Manual-review clarification.** A coding guide was written before inspecting the candidate pairs. It distinguishes repeated body, changed versions, shared templates, partial overlap, unrelated text and uncertain cases. This operationalises the frozen rule that H1 needs substantive repeated body; it does not change the automatic primary metric.
4. **Audit case selection.** When a corpus has more than 30 flagged candidate pairs, the 30 reviewed cases are chosen by a deterministic seed-balanced sample rather than simply taking the highest scores. This implementation detail was fixed before opening the local review sheet; it improves coverage of the three runs but remains a targeted audit, not a prevalence estimate.
5. **Unflagged audit subset.** The audit uses the ten highest-scoring unflagged candidate pairs per corpus (within the protocol's allowance of up to 30). This keeps the manual review feasible while examining likely threshold misses. It cannot measure full recall.
6. **Audit record.** Bekzat Rashiduly reviewed the 73 selected pairs in Appendix B. The labels and reasons are recorded against source row IDs. No second-reviewer agreement measure was collected, so category conclusions remain subject to reviewer disagreement.
7. **Independent reproduction evidence.** Nuradilet Mustafa reported a matching run on his own computer. His logs could not be transferred, and Appendix C leaves the machine, Python version, date and exact command fields pending rather than inventing them. A separate local rerun also matched all twelve deterministic outputs, but it does not replace Nuradilet Mustafa's missing logs.
8. **Administrative submission copy.** Student names and the honest upload-status field were added to a separate Appendix A submission copy after the local freeze. The original Appendix A is unchanged and still matches the hash in `local_freeze_record.json`. No sample, method, metric, or analysis decision changed.
